# Source Map

## Attached books

### Lucy Mayes — The Natural Pigment Handbook
Used for pigment-making structure, recipes, historical material context, pigment properties, particle morphology, lakes, binders, medium behaviour, copper pigments and responsible making.

### Color: A Practical Guide to Color and Its Uses in Art
Used for colour vocabulary, light/shadow, colour relativity, psychology, mood, schemes, composition, medium-specific painting, pigment quality chart and mixing guidance.

## Open references

### Colour Pigment Manufacturers’ Association / Colour Index
https://www.pigments.org/

Used to expand pigment definition, organic/inorganic classification, historical pigment development, Egyptian Blue, pigment particle/crystal behaviour, dispersion, optical behaviour and modern pigment families.

### Natural Pigments / Rublev Colours
https://www.naturalpigments.com/pigments/

Used as an open material/history reference for natural and historical pigments such as azurite, malachite, lapis lazuli and cinnabar, and for additional preparation/property context.

## Organization principle

The **Sources** page is the single place where the reference network is explained. Individual Library, History, Chemistry and Theory cards are intentionally kept readable and do not repeat source citations on every card.

Where attached-book instructions and open-web descriptions differ in presentation, the app uses a common structure: **Material → Chemistry → Properties → History → Making/Preparation → Lab connection**. The original source framing is retained where it is important to understanding the material.
